About ABDU’LLAH

A book marketing specialist helping authors find their way through a crowded publishing market.

I believe every good book deserves to find its readers. Writing the book is only half the work. Clear, targeted and patient marketing is what connects a finished manuscript to the people who will love it.

I work directly with self-published and traditionally published authors. My approach relies on reading the page the way a reader does, understanding the genre, and fixing what stands between the book and more readers.

Direct email communication

Customized strategy

How We Work Together

A simple four-step process.

  1. Initial outreach

    Email contactmeatabdullah@gmail.com with your book, genre, launch date and goals.

  2. Custom proposal

    I review your book’s position and reply within 24 hours with a plan and a quote.

  3. Campaign prep

    We improve copy, prepare visuals, build audience lists and set up ads before launch.

  4. Launch and reporting

    The campaign goes live, and you get regular email updates on what is happening.
